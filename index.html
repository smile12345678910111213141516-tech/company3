About Repowering Partners

We specialize in acquiring aging, distressed, and technically challenged solar projects where clear operational improvements can unlock substantial long-term value.

Our work combines commercial discipline and engineering depth. We review production history, contractual constraints, equipment health, and market context to build an actionable value-creation plan before close and an execution roadmap after close.

What We Target

Our investment lens is focused on projects where technical optimization, contract structuring, and operational discipline can significantly improve performance and reliability.

Aging & Legacy Assets

Projects reaching mid-to-late lifecycle stages that require inverter, module, or balance-of-plant modernization.

Distressed Situations

Assets with financial, contractual, or ownership complexity where disciplined underwriting can restore bankable value.

Output Underperformance

Sites falling below modeled production due to unresolved technical or O&M execution issues.

Our Transaction-to-Operations Process

A clear, repeatable process to convert underperforming sites into stable, high-confidence assets.

1. Screen: Rapid qualification based on age, output profile, and ownership context.
2. Underwrite: Integrated technical and financial diligence with downside scenario testing.
3. Acquire: Efficient transaction execution with transition planning in parallel.
4. Repower: Targeted interventions prioritized by yield impact and reliability uplift.
5. Optimize: Ongoing monitoring, KPI governance, and continuous performance improvement.
